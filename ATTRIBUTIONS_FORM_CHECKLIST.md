# Attributions Form — consistency checklist

**For whoever checks this:** you need the team's official iGEM Attributions Form open
(teams.igem.org → Attributions) alongside the wiki page at
`wiki/pages/attributions.html` in the repository at `~/Desktop/claremont-ca`, or live
at <https://2026.igem.wiki/claremont-ca/attributions>.

**Why this exists.** The wiki's attributions page was corrected on 2026-10-20/21 after
an audit found contradictions between it and the team's own lab records. Several
entries now say something different from what they said before. The official Form was
filled in earlier and has **not** been updated to match. Judges read both. Where they
disagree, the Form is the formal deliverable and the wiki is the public record, and
neither can be left saying something the other contradicts.

**The one thing to get right:** these entries describe what real people did. Do not
resolve a disagreement by guessing which version is true. Where the Form and the wiki
differ, flag it and ask the team which is correct. The only exception is item 1 below,
where the evidence is already settled.

---

## Part A — entries the wiki changed, which the Form probably still has in the old form

Check each of these on the Form. For each: does the Form say what the wiki now says?

### 1. Phage assembly and Pharokka annotation — the one that matters most

**The wiki now says** (Table 1, two separate rows):

> Phage assembly and Pharokka annotation — the canonical one, locus prefix `UUAIURNK_`,
> adopted 2026-07-16 and used for everything after it — **Dr. J. Cesar Ignacio Espinoza (PI)**
>
> Earlier phage assembly and annotation, superseded by the above — **Chun-Yu Chen, Yan Yan**

**It used to say** a single row: "Phage assembly and annotation — Chun-Yu Chen, Yan Yan".

**Why it changed.** The team's own questionnaire to the PI
(`~/Desktop/igem-2026/documents/2026-10-18_pi_contribution_questionnaire.md`) says:
"you produced the assembly and the Pharokka annotation with locus prefix `UUAIURNK_`,
which we adopted as the canonical CDS numbering on 2026-07-16 and used for everything
afterwards, **replacing our own**." The `UUAIURNK_` prefix appears throughout the Parts
page, so the entire CDS numbering rests on his annotation.

**What to check on the Form:** if it credits the assembly or the annotation to the
students without naming the PI, it contradicts the wiki and the team's own record.
This is the one case where the evidence is settled — the Form should be brought into
line, not the wiki.

**Related:** the wiki's Plasmidsaurus entry used to end "all assembly, annotation and
analysis was done by our team". It now reads "They provided the raw reads and nothing
else. The bacterial assemblies and all analysis are ours; the canonical phage assembly
and its Pharokka annotation are our PI's." Check the Form's Plasmidsaurus entry for the
old wording.

## Part B — things to verify on the Form regardless of the wiki

### 6. Is everyone on the Form also on the wiki, and vice versa?

Build the two lists and diff them. The wiki's Table 2 currently holds, in order:
Rafael Escobar · Maggie Chou · Lu (Lucy) Shi · Prof. Ran Libeskind-Hadas ·

The lab repo's copy of the Form lists 13 and omits Tolstorukov, James Manifold and
Twist Biosciences. Confirm which of those three are on the **official** Form.

### 7. Hudson Nguyen and Ryon Schmidt

The wiki says Hudson Nguyen did the same fundraising as Ryon Schmidt "on the same
terms", but only Ryon Schmidt appears in the wiki's Table 1 role list. If the Form
treats them identically, the wiki should too.

### 8. Ashley Armstrong's title

The wiki and the lab repo both call her **Director of Horticulture**. The Human
Practices page separately describes her and James Manifold as **trustees** of the
Garden. The team lead has said to treat the trustee description as confirmed, but if
the Form gives a different title, the three places should agree.

### 9. USC CARC versus the PI's cluster

The wiki credits USC CARC with "the high-performance computing on which genome assembly,
protein structure prediction and RBP detection were run", and separately says the PI ran
the structure predictions **on his own cluster**. Check whether these are the same
machine described two ways, or two different resources. As written a reader could take
them as contradicting each other. This needs a human answer, not a guess.

### 10. Does the Form's task list match what the wiki describes?

iGEM's Form assigns CRediT-style task categories (Investigation, Conceptualization,
Software, Fundraising, Project Administration, Writing, and so on). For each person,
check the categories ticked on the Form are consistent with what the wiki says they
did. A person credited on the wiki with bench work but ticked only "Writing" on the
Form is the kind of mismatch worth catching.

## How to report back

For each item: **agrees** / **Form needs updating** / **wiki needs updating** /
**needs the team to decide**, with the exact text from both sides quoted. Do not edit
the Form or the wiki without the team's say-so — except that the lab repo's stale copy
at `documents/2026-10-18_attribution_form.md` can be brought into line once the official
Form is settled, since it is an internal working file.
