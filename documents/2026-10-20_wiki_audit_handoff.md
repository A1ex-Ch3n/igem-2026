## 3. The method that works

1. Pull the claim from the **live page**, not the repo.
2. Find the **primary output** that produced it — a TSV, a CSV, a notebook's stored output.
   Not a summary document: several wrong numbers on this wiki trace to summary docs that were
   themselves written from the wiki.
3. Recompute. If the number cannot be reproduced from anything in the repository, **it is
   wrong until someone produces the file**. `178` had no source anywhere.
4. When a claim mixes two subjects, check each separately. `/model` said "KT2440 shares the
   most receptors — 68 at >=90%, max 99.4% — and has 71 phage records": the 68 and 99.4 were
   a different organism's.
