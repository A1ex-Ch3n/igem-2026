### J. Cesar Ignacio-Espinoza — Primary PI
Tasks: Conceptualization, Project Administration, Analysis, Software, Safety, Fundraising (Twist sponsorship), Writing
> (1) Software — carried out the computational design of the ancestral enzyme variants entirely himself: the GH5 phylogenetic inference, the ancestral-state reconstruction that produced the Node1, Node40 and Node281 sequences we expressed, and the protein-language-model scoring used to judge how well each designed sequence fits the enzyme class. This work is his, not the students'. He also produced the reference assembly and Pharokka annotation of phage 9-2-1 Φ8, which the team adopted as its canonical CDS numbering on 2026-07-16 in place of its own, and the AlphaFold/foldseek structural annotation of the virion plus the AlphaFold3 tail-cluster models that determined which fragment of CDS_0011 we cloned. (2) Conceptualization — set the scientific direction throughout: the GH5 depolymerase ancestral-reconstruction hypothesis, the reading of the receptor as an LPS sugar rather than a protein, the T7-like podophage (Latka group D) classification and the demand that we justify it by synteny, the design of all six expression constructs, and the withdrawal of the CDS_0010 glycosidase call. (3) Analysis — supplied the approach for a large share of the analyses the students implemented, including the genome assembly and annotation strategy, the RBP detection and receptor-classification work, the judgement that the RBP × receptor folding screen was not informative, and the O-antigen and resistant-mutant comparisons; reviewed and validated the students' computational results. (4) Project Administration — hosted the team in his KGI laboratory, supervised all wet-lab work under BSL-2, and provided reagents and the Laguna HPC allocation on which the team's entire computational workload ran. (5) Fundraising — arranged the Twist Bioscience sponsorship that supplied every plasmid in the project, and placed the synthesis and Plasmidsaurus sequencing orders. (6) Writing — senior author of the published genome announcement (doi 10.1128/mra.00793-26) and reviewer of the revision. (7) Safety — final review and submission of the Project Safety Form.
