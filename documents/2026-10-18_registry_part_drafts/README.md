## The distinction these drafts exist to protect

The three wild-type proteins are **three different things**, and the project has already
confused them once (the PI asked for "the enzymatic domain *within* the tail spike"; the
confirmed enzyme is a separate protein).

| | CDS_0010 | CDS_0011 | CDS_0012 |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | virion fiber, **function unknown** | **tail spike** — structural, virion-attached | **free enzyme**, not a virion component |
| Enzyme call | **none** | **β-helix depolymerase** | **GH5 glycoside hydrolase** |
| Evidence | one weak IspD template over 96 aa; ~800 aa with no template at all | **fold-level only** — AF3 has no template for res 111–941; the β-helix was called from the H-bond offset period, and there is no named homolog | **strongest in the project** — 4/4 AF3 templates are GH5 endoglucanases (1ECE with cellotetraose bound, 1VRX, 4TUF, 2ZUM), pLDDT 95–98 |
| Oligomer | monomer in the AF3 job | **trimer** (941 aa × 3, ≈ 238 Å long) | monomer |
| Shared | Calx-β anchor, 42 % identity / 107 aa with CDS_0011, anchors superpose at **0.50 Å** | same anchor as CDS_0010 | — |

So: **CDS_0011 is the tail spike whose body is a depolymerase; CDS_0012 is the
template-confirmed GH5 glycoside hydrolase and is a separate protein.** Never write that the
tail spike carries a GH5 domain — we have no evidence for that, and the two claims have very
different strength. CDS_0010 is called **unknown function**: the PI withdrew the glycosidase
call on 2026-07-29.

## Sequences

Registry-ready sequences are generated by
[`../../code/1018_construct_structures/registry_sequences.py`](../../code/1018_construct_structures/registry_sequences.py)
→ `code/output/1018_construct_structures/registry/registry_parts.{ffn,faa,tsv}`.

What the Twist sheets hold is an **insert**, not a part: it has its own stop codon but **no
start codon** (translation starts in pET-28a's `MGSSHHHHHHSSGLVPRGSHM` leader), and CDS11's
insert additionally carries a designed `GGSGGGGS`-His6-`ENLYFQ`-`GGGGSGGGGS` purification
linker. The drafts therefore submit **`ATG` + the protein's own CDS + stop**, with the vector
leader and that linker described in *design* rather than included in the sequence:

| Part | Registry sequence | Protein | Trimmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CDS10_WT` | 2445 bp | 814 aa | `ATG` added |
| `CDS11_WT` | 1917 bp | 638 aa | `ATG` added, 30 aa purification linker removed |
| `CDS12_WT` | 1089 bp | 362 aa | `ATG` added |

⚠️ **Sequence, authorship and licence lock permanently** when a part leaves Draft. Agree the
His6 policy once, for all six, before anyone clicks *Send for Screening*.
