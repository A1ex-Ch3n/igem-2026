# DNA Quantification & Prepare LB Broth, PIA Plates, Streak Colonies

**Source pages:** ![p15](images/p15.jpg) ![p16](images/p16.jpg)

## Objective
1. DNA quantification for the DNA extract from 2026-05-20.
2. Prepare 2× LB broth (500 mL each), Pseudomonas Isolation Agar (PIA) solution and made plates.
3. Streak isolated colonies from the plates Carol streaked on 2026-05-26.

## Procedure

### ① DNA quantification (Qubit)
1. Set up two assay tubes for the standards and one assay tube for each sample (we have 10).
2. Prepare the Qubit working solution by diluting the Qubit reagent 1:200 in Qubit buffer. Prepare 200 µL of working solution for each standard and sample.
3. Prepare the assay tubes:
   - **Standards:** 190 µL working solution + 10 µL of standard (200 µL total volume).
   - **Samples:** 190 µL working solution + 10 µL of DNA extract.
4. Vortex all tubes for 2–3 seconds.
5. Incubate the tubes for 2 minutes at room temperature.
6. Insert the tubes in the Qubit Fluorometer and take readings.

### ② Prepare LB broth and Pseudomonas Isolation Agar (PIA)
- **500 mL LB broth** (made 2 bottles):
  - 500 mL of MiniQ water
  - 12.5 g LB broth
- **500 mL PIA:**
  - 500 mL of MiniQ water
  - 12.5 g Pseudomonas Isolation Agar base medium
  - 10 mL of 100 % glycerol
- For the solutions above: mix and autoclave for 30 min.
- Added 8 mL of PIA solution to the plates. *(For 4 of them, we accidentally added 12 mL :C )*

### ③ Streak isolated colonies
- From the plates Carol did on 5-26, picked 2 isolated colonies from each plate and streaked them on the PIA plates. Each colony was streaked on half of the plate.

> *(Diagram on p.16: plate divided in half — colony **a** on the left, colony **b** on the right, each streaked in zig-zag pattern.)*
