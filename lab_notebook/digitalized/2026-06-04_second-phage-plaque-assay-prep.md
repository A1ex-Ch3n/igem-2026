# Second Phage Plaque Assay Prep; Culture Second Round Bacteria; Dispose Soil Enrichment

**Source pages:** ![p27](images/p27.jpg) ![p28](images/p28.jpg)

## Objective
1. Make second plaque assay in layered plates to hopefully get more phage and lysis.
2. Continue growing bacteria as we already have, making new cycles.
3. Discard soil enrichment as we already have phage & bacteria info from it — saves incubation space.

## Material
- **①** LB broth, agar, water bath, falcon tubes, pipette and tips, plates, **(scale) agar [with hand]** *(i.e., agar weighed by hand on a scale)*.
- **②** LB broth, tubes, pipette & 5 mL tips.
- **③** Bleaching solution.

## Procedure ① — Second plaque assay (layered plates)
1. **Prepare 0.7 % LB + Agar Solution:** 3 mL × 30 plates = 90 mL solution. We made 100 mL LB + 0.7 g agar and sent to autoclave for 30 min in a 200 mL bottle.
2. While waiting, we prepared the water bath to 50 °C.
3. We put 3 mL of media, 250 µL of actively grown bacteria, and 10 µL of phage (8, 9, 10) into a new falcon tube in the water bath. *(While doing this: turn on alcohol burner.)*
4. Pour liquid in the falcon tube to a ~~soft~~ **hard** agar plate, and discard the falcon tube. Make sure new agar covers all surfaces. *(Phage titrate stored at 4 °C.)*
5. Repeat steps 3–4 for the rest of ~9 plates. **30 plates total in 3 groups**; each group has 10 tubes of each bacteria culture with **ϕ 8**, the other is the same except with **ϕ 9**, the last is for **ϕ 10**.

   > **Plate-labelling convention** *(left-margin illustration on p.27):* each plate is marked with three rows —
   > 1. **Date** (e.g. `26/6/4`)
   > 2. **Phage ID** — `ϕ<n>`, where `n` is the soil enrichment the phage filtrate came from
   > 3. **Bacteria label** (e.g. `8-8-2`, in `sample_site.duplicate.plate_half` format)
6. Wait until the last 3 plates solidify (10 min), put back to incubator upside-down lid. *(The middle of … — sentence trails off in source.)*

## Procedure ② — Culture second round bacteria
1. Turn on the alcohol burner and label 10 new tubes as before with new dates. *(Margin note: `8-5-2  6/14` — bacteria source label on the tube cap, next-step date on the bottle.)*
2. Add 5 mL of LB broth to 10 new bacteria culture tubes.
3. Add 50 µL of first tube of bacteria to the new tube with corresponding label and mix well (1:100 for bacteria culturing).
4. Repeat steps 2–3 for the rest of the 9 tubes of bacteria.
5. Put the rack back to the incubator with tilted slope to maximize surface area to oxygen.

## Procedure ③ — Dispose soil enrichment
- Since we've had phage & bacteria from the soil enrichment, we no longer need those 6 flasks and dispose of them.
- For media disposal, we add bleaching to it and after finishing today's work, we poured the liquid in each flask in the sink, and washed those flasks.
- We then flush them through DI water.
