## Procedure

### ① Bacteria culture (p37) — see [[single_colony_inoculation]]
1. Set up materials (LB tubes, sterile loops, gloves) in the safety cabinet — same as the previous inoculation round.
2. **Select and mark 3 colonies on each plate.** Label each culture tube with the plate / colony ID. `[TBD: exact label scheme — p37 reads "00/0 / plate number (D in E, C)".]`
3. Use the loop to **scrape the colony surface** — carefully, not too deep — and dissolve it into the corresponding LB tube. **Change the loop for each colony.**
4. **Repeat for the remaining tubes (~30 total — "the next 27 tubes" + the first 3).** Put the rack into the incubator as a fixed group. `[TBD: confirm total tube count.]`

> *Flagged inference (confirm):* the source plates are the **10 revived stocks from [2026-06-08](2026-06-08_metagenomic-extraction-dna-concentration-quantification.md) ①** (the likely *P. aeruginosa* candidate panel) — 10 plates × 3 colonies ≈ 30 tubes. Not stated explicitly on the legible part of p37.

### ② Phage isolation (p37–p38) — see [[phage_isolation]]
1. **Put plastic equipment under UV light for 10 min** (sterilize).
2. **Pipette yesterday's [[soil_enrichment_phage]] broth into 2 mL microcentrifuge tubes** — **4 tubes per enrichment**, for the **6 phage enrichments: #3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10** (confirmed; set up on 6-8, same sites as the 6-1 enrichment). 6 enrichments × 4 tubes = **24 tubes**.
3. **Centrifuge the 24 tubes in 2 runs, ~5 min**, in the small (micro)centrifuge. **A higher speed than last time (the [6-2](2026-06-02_inoculation-phage-isolation.md) run) gives a cleaner supernatant.** `[TBD: exact speed — p37 reads "5×g", which is a misread of a higher rpm/×g.]`
4. **Draw the supernatant of each enrichment** with a pipette into a new tube — **fresh pipette tip per enrichment**.
5. **Syringe the liquid through a 0.22 µm filter into a new 2 mL microcentrifuge tube; label each.** These are the **phage filtrate** (`ϕ<n>`), one per enrichment. *(Single 0.22 µm filtration, following the [6-2](2026-06-02_inoculation-phage-isolation.md) procedure — confirmed.)*
6. For each filtrate (**ϕ3, ϕ5, ϕ6, ϕ8, ϕ9, ϕ10**): ~**2–3 mL** each `[TBD: photo unclear]` → **4 °C fridge**, to be diluted for the later plaque assay.

> [!note] Observation (p38)
> The supernatant came out **cleaner / smoother and less smelly** than the previous round — attributed to the **higher centrifuge speed**.
