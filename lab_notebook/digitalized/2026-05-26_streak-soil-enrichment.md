# Streak Soil Enrichment on PMM Plates

**Source page:** ![p14](images/p14.jpg)

## Objective
Streak 10 soil enrichments onto the PMM plates using the three-phase streak method.

> *(Diagram on p.14: circular plate with three regions labelled "most intense", "less, medium", "dilute" showing decreasing density of streaks.)*

## Material
- 60 plates (10 plates per sample)
- 6 inoculating loops *(size not recorded this run; **probably 1 µL** — same loops as the [2026-05-22](2026-05-22_streak-test-trial.md) streak-test trial, which used 1 µL. ==CONFIRM with Carol==)*

## Procedure
1. Separated per plate into 3 blocks and streaked in each block in a decreasing density.
2. Streak by dipping a loop in enrichment and then streak on the surface of the plate.
   - Make sure the streaking doesn't tear the agar.
   - In this experiment, the streaking should overlap with each other.
   - The loops should be sterilized.
3. The expected result is the isolated single colonies to be inoculated.
4. Put bacteria back to the incubator.
