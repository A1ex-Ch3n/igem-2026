## Objective (p82)
1. **Prepare 500 mL of LB broth with milliQ water and 500 mL of LB agar (1 %).**
2. **Prepare the containers (flasks) for growing bacteria** by washing, rinsing with DI, and autoclaving them.
3. **Plate the agar with the antibiotic kanamycin only.**
4. **Streak the DH10-B-like bacteria from Twist. Store at 37 °C.**

## Procedure (p82–p83)
- **Kanamycin plates are at 30 µg/mL**, so for 500 mL:
  - `30 × 500 = 15 000` µg **= 15 mg**
  - stock **kanamycin 50 mg/mL** → `15 mg → 0.3 mL = 300 µL`
  - ★ margin note: **"Remember to label."**
- **Wait until the LB agar *and* broth in the bottle are ~40 °C before adding the kanamycin** — margin note *"kanamycin will not denature if below 220 °C?"* with the answer **"BOTH!"** written across it.
- **Pipette 8 mL of LB agar + kanamycin per plate**, under the vacuum area of the Bunsen burner.
- **Streak using the small blue loops on the plates: 6 constructs from Twist** (the black tubes are in −80 °C).
- **Labelled the plates to correspond to the Twist tubes.**
- **Stored at 37 °C, turned upside down.**
- **The rest of the LB + kanamycin plates are at 4 °C.**
