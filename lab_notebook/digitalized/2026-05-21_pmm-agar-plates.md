# Prepare Agar Plate with Pseudomonas Minimum Media (PMM)

**Source page:** ![p11](images/p11.jpg)

## Objective
Prepare PMM solution and PMM agar plates to select bacteria that can grow on PMM.

## Material — PMM (per 1 L)
- 900 mL H₂O
- phosphate buffer
	- 2.07 g Monosodium Phosphate (NaH₂PO₄·H₂O)
	- 2.61 g Dipotassium Phosphate (K₂HPO₄)  
- Adjust pH to 6.8 using 1 N NaOH or HCl
- 0.05 g FeCl₃ as mineral base
- 15 g agar for solidification

> **Note:** Autoclave liquid for 45 min; separate into 2 bottles before autoclaving.

## Procedure
1. Prepared 1000 mL PMM solution following the protocol (Rafael's notebook).
2. Sterilized PMM solution in the autoclave for 45 min.
3. Pipetted 7 mL PMM solution to each plate and made 40 PMM agar plates.
