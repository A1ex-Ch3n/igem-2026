# DNA Extraction and Soil Enrichment

**Source page:** ![p10](images/p10.jpg)

## Objective
1. Extract metagenomic DNA sequence from the 10 collected soil samples.
2. Prepare soil enrichment for bacteria & phage to grow.

## Material
- DNA extraction kit
- Flask
- Scale

## Procedure
1. Followed the guidance for soil sample in the kit; did 10 groups of DNA extraction.
2. Labeled the DNA filtrate as Metagenomic Sample 1–10 with date and put into the 4 °C fridge.
3. Prepared 6 soil-sample enrichments in a flask: **#2, #5, #6, #8, #9, #10** *(of the 10 total sites collected on 2026-05-19)*.
   - **Ingredients:** see [`protocol/PMM_ProtocoL (1).docx`](../../protocol/PMM_ProtocoL%20(1).docx) (Pseudomonas Minimal Medium recipe). Follow the stock-solution preparation and the 1 L preparation procedure (phosphate buffer → mineral base → carbon/nitrogen sources after autoclave).
