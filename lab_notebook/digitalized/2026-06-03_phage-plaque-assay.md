# Soft Plate for Phage, Phage Plaque Assay, Phage Enrichment Filtrate, Bacteria Pellets

**Source pages:** ![p24](images/p24.jpg) ![p25](images/p25.jpg) ![p26](images/p26.jpg)

## Objective
1. Prepare 0.7 % LB agar for mixing with bacteria sample and soft-plating.
2. Divide phage buffer and PBS into smaller tubes for future use to avoid contamination caused by directly pipetting from the big bottles.
3. Prepare bacteria plates from 2026-06-02's bacteria sample.
4. Prepare new bacteria stock from 2026-06-02's bacteria sample to keep growing.
5. Plaque assay.

## Procedure

### ① Prepare 0.7 % LB agar
For 0.7 % LB agar: added 50 mL LB broth and 0.35 g agar powder, mixed and autoclaved for 1 hr.

### ② Plaque assay (soft-plating)
- Mixed 5 mL LB agar with **500 µL** of each bacteria sample (`6-3-2`, `10-10-2`, `10-9-2`, `9-2-2`, `8-3-2`, `8-2-2`, `9-2-1`, `8-5-1`).
- For each sample, put it into 2 plates with **2750 µL** mixture.
- Labeled the plates into 3 parts, so for each bacteria sample we could test 6 different phages in total.
- Dropped 0.2 mL of phage to the according part of the plate (phages **3 / 5 / 6 / 8 / 9 / 10**).

> *(Schematic on p.25 — plate divided into 3 wedges labelled ϕA / ϕB / ϕC; drop 2 mL of phage filtrate per phase; wait until phage is absorbed; store in 30 °C incubator upside down with cap on bottom.)*
>
> Plan totals: **18 plates, 27 bacteria (10 kinds/samples), 54 sites for testing, 6 ϕ enrichments.**

### ③ Bacteria pellets from 2026-06-02's bacteria sample
1. Add #1 & #2 of tubes from 06-02's sample into a 15 mL falcon tube.
2. Centrifuge at 3000 rpm for 15 min; pour supernatant to waste.
3. Wash with 5 mL of PBS & repeat step 2. Repeat twice.
4. Wash with 1.5 mL PBS, transfer to 2 mL Eppendorf. Centrifuge at 10,000 g for 10 min. Pour the supernatant to waste.
5. Store the pellets.

### Experiment notes (for bacteria-sample sequencing)
- 3000 rpm 15 min for centrifuge for bacteria sample for sequencing (falcon tube).
- Wash bacteria pellets with PBS for 2 times (balanced).
- 10,000 g 10 min for centrifuge for bacteria sample for sequencing (Eppendorf 2 mL).
- −20 °C for bacteria pellets.
