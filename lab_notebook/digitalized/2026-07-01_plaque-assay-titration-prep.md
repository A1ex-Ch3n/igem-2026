## Summary (p68)
- **Transfer colonies from plates to LB media** for later plaque-assay use (plates are from [6/30](2026-06-30_streak-24-bacteria-agar-plates.md)).
- **Combine phage lysate** → one pooled **`9-2-1` Φ** and one pooled **`9-6-2` Φ**.
- **Serial 1:1 dilution** of the pooled lysate, **from 1:2 to 1:2048** — **200 µL : 200 µL each time**.

## Plaque assay (p68) — see [[plaque_assay_layered_plate]]

| Phage | Dilutions plated |
|---|---|
| `9-2-1` Φ8 | 1:8, 1:64, 1:256, 1:512, 1:1024, 1:2048 |
| `9-6-2` Φ8 | 1:8, 1:64, 1:256, 1:512, 1:1024, 1:2048 |

**Per plate:** **3 mL 0.7 % LB agar + 300 µL bacteria + 100 µL phage** (12 plates).

## Storage
- **Phage lysate & dilutions → 4 °C.**
- **Plates → 30 °C incubator.**

## Result
Read out on [2026-07-02](2026-07-02_host-range-test-start.md): **no plaques / clear spots** at any of these dilutions → the series is pushed further (1:1024 → 1:16384).
