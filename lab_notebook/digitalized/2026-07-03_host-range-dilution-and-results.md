## Procedure (p70)
- Host-range test uses a **dilution factor of 1:3000** for the Φ solution.
- **To make 3 mL of 1:3000:**
  1. **10 µL Φ solution + 90 µL Φ buffer** → mix
  2. **2990 µL Φ buffer + 10 µL of ①** → mix   *(3000 µL − 10 µL)*

## Experiment note (p70)
- Also made **~80 new 5 mL 1 % agar plates**.
- **Plates → incubator, upside down.**
- **Rest of the 1:3000 dilution (~1200 µL each) → 4 °C.**
