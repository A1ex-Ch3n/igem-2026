# Prepare LB Agar Plates and Phage Buffer; Streak Bacteria; Phage Enrichment

**Source pages:** ![p20](images/p20.jpg) ![p21](images/p21.jpg)

## Objective
1. Prepare LB agar, LB broth, phage buffer for future use.
2. Make enrichment for phage in the soil sample.
3. Streak bacteria stock that we put in the freezer last time on the plates.

## Procedure

### ① Prepare media and buffer
**a. 500 mL LB agar** (concentration 1 %):
- 500 mL of DI water
- 12.5 g of LB broth
- 5 g of agar powder

**b. 500 mL LB broth:**
- 500 mL of DI water
- 12.5 g of LB broth

**c. 1000 mL phage buffer:**
- 1 L of MiniQ water
- 1.21 g of Tris
- 2.46 g of MgSO₄
- 3.97 g of NaCl
- 0.15 g of CaCl₂

For all the solutions above: mix and autoclave for 1 hr.

### ② Phage enrichment from soil
- Picked soil samples **#3, #5, #6, #8, #9, #10**.
- For each of the 6 flasks, added:
  - 1 g of soil sample
  - 25 mL of LB broth
- Shook thoroughly.

### ③ Streak bacteria stock
- Took out the bacteria stock we froze last Friday and placed them in an ice bucket.
- Stocks: `8-2-2`, `10-9-2`, `9-6-2`, `8-3-2`, `10-10-2`, `8-5-1`, `9-2-1`, `9-2-2`, `8-5-2`, `6-3-2`.
- Streaked each bacteria sample on the **8 mL** LB agar plates we made in procedure ④.

### ④ Plate LB agar
- Plated 8 mL agar plates × 10, and used up all of the LB agar to make 8 mL plates.
