## Objective & procedure (p84)
1. Put **falcon tubes, rack, sponge, Sharpies and tips** in the safety cabinet and **sterilized for 5 min**.
2. For the plates streaked on **9/1**, **picked one isolated bacteria colony for each**.
   *(**There's nothing growing on Ⓚ `CDS10 WT xAG`.**)*
3. **Labelled the 5 tubes**; for each tube added **5 mL LB + Kan** and used small blue loops to mix
   the colony into the LB.
4. **Stored the tubes in the incubator.**
5. **Parafilmed the plates and stored at 4 °C just in case.**
