# Pellets for the Gel; Re-streak and Restart the Transformed Cultures

**Source page:** ![p95](images/p95.jpg)

> [!note] Photo legibility
> p95 is legible throughout.

## Objective (p95)
- **Restart: streak transformed cell stocks to get new active colonies, one per construct.**
- **For current cultures, get pellets because the OD value is low after growing from yesterday
  overnight, and re-culture with new-made LB + Ⓚ to reactivate them.**
- **Run and stain a gel for the prepared pellets for `e1`, `e40`, `e281` with and without IPTG, to
  check if the proteins express successfully.**

## Procedure (p95)
1. **Streaked transformed `e1`, `e40`, `e81`, `CDS11` and `CDS12` with 8 duplicates each
   (total 5 × 8 = 40 plates).**
2. **Collected pellets from yesterday's 6 cultures by centrifuge, 4000 rpm, 10 min.**
   **Resuspended the pellet with 3 mL 1× PBS buffer and mixed using a pipette.**
3. **Added 100 mL of new LB + Ⓚ and 1 mL of each cell solution. Measured OD for the initial value.**
