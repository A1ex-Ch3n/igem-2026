# Streak Test Trial & Prepare PMM Plates

**Source page:** ![p12](images/p12.jpg)

## Objective
1. Learn how to streak bacteria sample from the soil enrichment on the agar plates.
2. Prepare Pseudomonas Minimum Media (PMM) plates.

## Procedure

### ① Practice streaking
- Used 1 µL inoculation loops and violet indicator.
- Each of us practiced on 5 plates.
- For each of the 3 streaks, make sure the next streak starts at the point where the previous streak ends.
- We didn't do overlap, but PI wanted us to do so.

> *(Diagram on p.12: circle plate divided into three regions — successive streaks drawn in a zig-zag pattern with each starting from the end of the previous.)*

### Trial round
- Used 1 µL blue inoculation loops.
- Alex used soil enrichment **#8**; Olivia used soil enrichment **#10**.
- Duplicated to control for random variables. If all 4 plates failed, it could be the loop's problem.

**Plates streaked:**
- Alex: `8.1.1`, `8.1.2`, `8.2.1`, `8.2.2`
- Olivia: `10.1.1`, ~~`10.1.2`~~, `10.1.3`, `10.1.4` *(Olivia forgot to change the loop :( )*

> Label format: `sample_site . duplicate_number . plate_section`. Each sample site has 10 plates; each plate is split in half; the 3rd number = which half (1 = left, 2 = right).

### ② Prepare PMM plates
- Added 12 mL PMM solution to each plate. While they were solidifying, turned on UV light to keep sterile.
