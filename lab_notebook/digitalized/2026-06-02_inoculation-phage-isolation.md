# Inoculate Bacteria from LB Plates & Phage Isolation

**Source pages:** ![p22](images/p22.jpg) ![p23](images/p23.jpg)

## Objective
1. Culture 3 colonies per plate into the LB tube to make bacteria grow happily in a single room.
2. Isolate phage from soil enrichment to prepare for purification.

## Material
- Loop, 30 tubes, racks
- 12 falcon tubes, filter, syringe

## Procedure ① — Inoculation
Same as [2026-05-28](2026-05-28_inoculation-2nd-dna-extraction.md), except **3 colonies per LB plate** for this experiment.

## Procedure ② — Phage isolation
1. Pipetted 12 mL soil enrichment into a falcon tube and labeled the corresponding number. Repeat.
2. Used centrifuge to centrifuge 6 falcon tubes at 2800 rpm for 5 min.
3. Took supernatant by syringing it (5 mL volume) per time and transferred to a new falcon tube using a filter.
4. The filtrate is the isolated phage. Put it into the fridge.

**Flow:**
- Start with 3× LB tubes; → distribute into **conical falcon tubes (10 mL)**, one falcon tube per sample.
- 50 µL into new LB-fresh + 5 mL → incubator.

**In parallel — soft agar prep:**
- 0.7 % LB agar = 50 mL LB broth + 0.35 g agar → autoclave.
- *Keep at warm water bath, 50 °C.*

**Plating (per plate):**
- 250 µL bacteria sample + 3 mL of 0.7 % LB agar + 10 µL of ϕ titrate (kept in fridge).
- Mix and plate.

**Scale:** 10 bacteria × 6 phage filtrate → 60 plates.

**Reminder:** divide PBS & phage buffer into smaller tubes for future use to avoid contamination caused by directly pipetting from the big bottle.

**Centrifuge instructions** *(to be applied next experiment)*:
