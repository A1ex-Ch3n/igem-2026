# Inoculation and 2nd DNA Extraction

**Source page:** ![p17](images/p17.jpg)

## Objective
1. Inoculate 16 bacteria colonies from the 10 samples (ideally 2 colonies per plate) to LB broth tubes.
2. Conduct DNA extraction for the second time and aim for a successful metagenomic DNA extraction.

## Material
- 16 tubes, rack, loop, LB broth, pipette

## Procedure for ①
1. Pipetted 5 mL LB broth to each tube. Labeled each tube corresponding to the plate's number.
2. Selected and marked a single colony from the PIA plate.
3. Picked that one colony on the loop and dissolved it in half of the LB broth.
4. Repeated steps 2–3 for the rest of the plates. *(Be careful of contamination!)*
5. Put the bacteria to the incubator.

## Procedure for ②
Same as the 1st DNA Extraction (see [2026-05-20](2026-05-20_dna-extraction-soil-enrichment.md)).
