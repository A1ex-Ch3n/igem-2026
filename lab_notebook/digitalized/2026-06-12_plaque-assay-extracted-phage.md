## Procedure (p43)

### ② Pick phage from the 06/10 plaques — see [[phage_purification]]
Picked the **`6-8-1` Φ3** and **`8-1-2` Φ6** plaques off the 06/10 plates into phage buffer (the **extracted phage** used below).
