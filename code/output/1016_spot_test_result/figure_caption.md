# Figure — first spot test of the expressed CDS_0012-family proteins

Files: `fig_spot_test.png` (300 dpi), `fig_spot_test.pdf`. Made by `code/1016_spot_test_result.ipynb`, section 6.

**Figure X. Proof-of-concept spot test of three expressed CDS_0012-family proteins on seven *Pseudomonas* lawns.**
Each strain was grown as a double-layer lawn (200 µL culture in 3 mL soft agar). Each plate received five 7 µL drops:
phage Φ8 stock (positive control), 1× PBS (negative control), purified e1 (1:10), and crude lysates of e40 and e281
(1:10). Drops were spotted on 12 Oct 2026 and photographed on 16 Oct. R1 = Φ8-resistant isolate picked from the
parent strain.
**(A)** Each drop, cropped from the plate photograph without colour correction. Rows are strains; columns are treatments.
**(B)** Colonies counted inside each drop by automated image segmentation (touching colonies count as one, so these
are lower bounds). One point per strain. Bars show medians, with 9-1-1 excluded.
No drop produced a clear lysis zone. The trend is in colony growth inside the drop. Colonies grew in every PBS drop
(median 14.5), so they were introduced with the drop liquid and are not survivors of lysis. In the Φ8 drops,
colonies were almost entirely absent (median 0). In the purified e1 drops, they were strongly reduced (median 1;
0–4 per strain). The crude e40 and e281 lysates fell between these two levels (medians 10 and 9). This first
experiment is consistent with the purified e1 preparation carrying an activity that suppresses bacterial growth,
comparable to the phage control. Only e1 was purified, and the drops were diluted in different buffers, so the
experiment does not yet separate a protein effect from a preparation or buffer effect.
\*9-1-1: the assignment of drops to treatments on this plate is not yet confirmed (open circles in B). The 3 objects
counted in the 9-6-2 Φ8 drop are reflections at the drop edge, not colonies.

## 中文說明（wiki 用）

**第一次 spot test（proof of concept）。** 7 株 *Pseudomonas* lawn，每盤點 5 滴（各 7 µL）：Φ8（陽性對照）、PBS（陰性對照）、純化 e1（1:10），以及 e40、e281 crude lysate（1:10）。

結果：
- 沒有任何一滴出現透明的裂解區。看得到的趨勢是**滴裡長出來的菌落數**。
- PBS 滴每盤都長菌落（中位數 14.5），表示菌是跟著液體帶進來的。
- Φ8 滴幾乎沒有菌落（中位數 0）。
- **純化 e1 滴的菌落也大幅減少**（中位數 1），程度接近 Φ8。
- e40、e281 crude lysate 介於兩者之間（中位數 10、9）。

初步看來，純化的 e1 有抑制細菌生長的活性。但只有 e1 是純化的，各滴的稀釋 buffer 也不同，所以還不能把蛋白本身的效果跟 buffer、製備過程的效果分開。

## Do not claim from this figure

- That the proteins were expressed. A spot test cannot show that. Expression evidence has to come from gels or a
  western blot. So far the gels give only candidate bands, and none is assigned yet (0929: CDS12 IPTG-only band at
  40–43 kDa; e1 band at 45–50 kDa).
- That e40 or e281 are inactive. They are crude lysates and are not comparable to purified e1.
- Anything about R1 resistance. Φ8 gave no clearing on the parents either.

## Open before reuse

- What each 1:10 was diluted in, and whether the PBS was sterile.
- Whether a confluent lawn formed (no clearing anywhere, even with Φ8).
- Whether the Φ8 sector had two drops (two rings on most plates; the outer ring was scored).
- The 9-1-1 sector map.
