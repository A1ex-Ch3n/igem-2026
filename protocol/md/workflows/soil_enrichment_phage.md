# Soil Enrichment — Phage (LB-based)

## Purpose
Inoculate soil into plain LB broth so the bacteria native to that soil grow up — and any phages of those bacteria amplify on them. The resulting enrichment is centrifuged and filtered ([[phage_isolation]]) to give a cell-free phage filtrate.

The medium is intentionally **LB**, not PMM — for the phage track we want the natural soil bacteria as hosts (the broadest possible phage capture), not just *Pseudomonas*.

## Procedure
1. For each soil sample to enrich:
   1. Add **1 g** of soil to a flask.
   2. Add **25 mL** of [[lb_broth]].
2. Shake thoroughly to suspend.
