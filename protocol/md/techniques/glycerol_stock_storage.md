# Glycerol Stock Preparation & −80 °C Storage

## Purpose
Mix an overnight liquid culture 1:1 with 50 % glycerol so the final tube contains ~25 % glycerol — enough cryoprotection to survive −80 °C freezing — and store the labelled tube in the −80 °C freezer for long-term archive and future revival.

## Selection criteria — which cultures to bank
*(Per the 5-29 protocol and confirmed verbally by user; see [[od600_measurement]] for how the OD reading is taken.)*

- **Hard threshold:** raw A₆₀₀ ≥ 0.6 at full (undiluted) concentration. Below this, the culture is too dilute to bank reliably.
- **Best candidate range:** **A₆₀₀ between 0.6 and 1.0** — likely still in or near log phase. Above 1.0 risks being past log phase (no 24-h OD curve has been done to confirm log-phase A₆₀₀).
- **Backup banking:** all tested cultures (~20 on 5-29) are *also* banked at **−20 °C** as a backup, regardless of OD.

## Quick-lookup table — mix ratios

| Final tube volume | Bacteria culture | [[glycerol_50pct]] | Final glycerol % |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.3 mL** (5-29 recipe) | **650 µL** | **650 µL** | 25 % |

## Materials & equipment
- Eppendorf tubes (1.5 or 2 mL), pre-labelled with stock ID
- Pipette (1000 µL) + tips
- [[glycerol_50pct]] (prepared same day or stored)
- Overnight bacteria culture(s) — [[single_colony_inoculation]] output
- Ice bucket (recommended for transit)
- −80 °C freezer (long-term)
- −20 °C freezer (backup)

## Reagents
- [[glycerol_50pct]] — 1:1 mix partner
- Overnight bacterial culture (output of [[single_colony_inoculation]])

## Procedure
1. Label each Eppendorf with the stock ID (`sample_site.duplicate.plate_half` format — see [2026-05-22](../../../lab_notebook/digitalized/2026-05-22_streak-test-trial.md) for label convention).
2. Pipette **650 µL of bacteria culture** into the Eppendorf.
3. Pipette **650 µL of [[glycerol_50pct]]** into the same Eppendorf.
4. Cap tightly, invert several times to mix.
5. Transfer immediately to the **−80 °C freezer** for archive stocks (those in the 0.6–1.0 OD band).
6. For backup banking of all tested cultures, transfer to the **−20 °C freezer**.

## Notes / common deviations
- On 5-29 the team stored everything tested (~20 cultures) at **−20 °C** rather than choosing only the best — this is conservative and recommended while the −80 °C selection criteria are being refined.
- Once stored, never thaw and refreeze — pull a stock once, scrape from the surface, return to the freezer immediately if there's still material on it; otherwise discard.

## Checklist — where do the outputs go?
- [ ] Archive glycerol stocks (A₆₀₀ 0.6–1.0) → **−80 °C freezer** (long-term).
- [ ] Backup glycerol stocks (all tested cultures) → **−20 °C freezer**.
- [ ] Excess [[glycerol_50pct]] working solution → 4 °C fridge for short-term reuse, or [[media_disposal]].
