# PIA (Pseudomonas Isolation Agar)

## Purpose
PIA further restricts growth to *Pseudomonas* species relative to [[pmm]]. Used downstream of PMM streaking: isolated colonies from PMM plates are re-streaked onto PIA to verify *Pseudomonas* identity by colony morphology and pigment before downstream liquid culture and sequencing.

## Quick-lookup table

| Final volume | MiniQ water | PIA base medium | 100 % glycerol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **500 mL** | **500 mL** | **12.5 g** | **10 mL** |

## Materials & equipment
- 500 mL (or larger) autoclave-safe bottle
- Scale
- Pipette (for glycerol)
- Autoclave
- Autoclave indicator tape
- Petri dishes

## Reagents
- Pseudomonas Isolation Agar base medium (commercial powder). `[TBD: brand / catalog number]`
- 100 % glycerol
- MiniQ water

## Procedure

> [!important] Label as you prepare
> Label every bottle and tube with its **substance, volume, and date** (e.g. `PIA — 500 mL — 2026-06-08`) before it goes on a shelf or into the fridge.

1. Add 500 mL of MiniQ water to the bottle.
2. Weigh out 12.5 g of PIA base medium and add to the water.
3. Pipette 10 mL of 100 % glycerol into the bottle.
4. Cap loosely with autoclave tape (Autoclave Indicator Tape) over the cap, then autoclave for **30 min**.
5. After autoclave, dispense **8 mL per plate** (notebook 5-27 standard).
   - *Caveat from 5-27:* "For 4 of them, we accidentally added 12 mL :C" — pay attention to pour volume.

## Notes / common deviations
- The 8 mL pour volume gives roughly **60 plates per 500 mL bottle** (under-fills and over-fills factored in).
- Glycerol in the recipe acts as a wettability / texture additive — it's part of the commercial PIA formulation, not a substitute for autoclaving.

## Checklist — where do the outputs go?
- [ ] Poured PIA plates (after solidification) → **4 °C fridge**, sealed plastic bag.
- [ ] Bulk autoclaved PIA if not poured same day → **4 °C fridge**, cap tight.
- [ ] Unused PIA at end of project → [[media_disposal]].
