# 50 % Glycerol Solution

## Purpose
A 1:1 dilution of 100 % glycerol in water used as the cryoprotectant for bacterial glycerol stocks. Mixing equal volumes with an overnight liquid culture gives a final 25 % glycerol concentration in the stock tube — the standard range for cell viability after −80 °C freezing.

## Quick-lookup table

| Final volume | 100 % glycerol | MiniQ water |
|---|---|---|
| 10 mL | 5 mL | 5 mL |
| 50 mL | 25 mL | 25 mL |

## Materials & equipment
- Bottle or conical tube, sized to final volume
- Serological pipette or graduated cylinder (glycerol is viscous — use the larger tip)

## Reagents
- 100 % glycerol
- MiniQ water

## Procedure
1. Measure equal volumes of 100 % glycerol and MiniQ water.
2. Combine in the bottle. Glycerol is viscous — invert / swirl thoroughly to mix.
3. Use the same day, or store at **room temperature** (same day) / **4 °C fridge** (short-term reuse over a few days).
