# LB Broth

## Purpose
Standard rich-medium broth used throughout the project: as a carrier for bacterial inoculation, as the broth phase of [[lb_agar]], for the soft-agar overlay in plaque assays, and for resuspending bacteria for OD measurements.

## Quick-lookup table

| Final volume | Water (DI or MiniQ) | LB broth powder | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 mL | 100 mL | 2.5 g | for small / single-tube prep |
| **500 mL** | **500 mL** | **12.5 g** | project standard (5-27, 6-1) |
| 1 L | 1 L | 25 g | for large-batch days |

> **Concentration:** 25 g LB broth / L (≈ 2.5 % w/v) — derived from the 12.5 g / 500 mL recipe used on both 5-27 and 6-1. `[TBD: confirm this matches the brand-recommended ratio printed on the LB-broth canister.]`

> **Autoclave cycle used in notebook:** 30 min ([5-27](../../../lab_notebook/digitalized/2026-05-27_dna-quantification-pia-plates.md)) / 1 hr ([6-1](../../../lab_notebook/digitalized/2026-06-01_lb-agar-phage-buffer.md)) — see Procedure step 3 for which to use.

## Materials & equipment
- Bottle (500 mL or larger, autoclave-safe)
- Scale (for weighing LB powder)
- Autoclave
- Autoclave indicator tape

## Reagents
- LB broth powder. `[TBD: brand / catalog number]`
- DI water (5-27 used MiniQ; 6-1 used DI). `[TBD: pick one for the standard protocol.]`

## Procedure

> [!important] Label as you prepare
> Label every bottle and tube with its **substance, volume, and date** (e.g. `LB broth — 500 mL — 2026-06-08`) before it goes on a shelf or into the fridge.

1. Add the water volume to the bottle (see table).
2. Weigh out the LB broth powder and add it to the water.
3. Cap loosely with autoclave tape (Autoclave Indicator Tape) over the cap, then autoclave.
   - 30 min was used on 5-27.
   - 1 hr was used on 6-1.
   - `[TBD: standardize one autoclave cycle for routine use.]`
4. Allow to cool to handling temperature before use; keep cap snug once cool.

## Notes / common deviations
- On 5-27 the team made **2 × 500 mL bottles** in the same autoclave run.
- LB broth is the broth phase of [[lb_agar]] — for solid plates, agar is added before autoclaving (see [[lb_agar]]).
- For the soft-agar overlay used in plaque assay, the broth is made fresh on the day (see 6-3 and 6-4 entries).
